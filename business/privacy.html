Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

TrustLens is an AI-powered content trust analysis app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect user information.

1. Information We Collect

TrustLens may collect the following information:

  • Google account information used for sign-in, such as email address and profile name.
  • Content submitted by users for analysis, such as text, images, audio files, video URLs, or documents.
  • Analysis history stored locally or through Firebase services.
  • Device and usage information for app functionality, security, and analytics.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide AI-based trust analysis features.
  • Manage user sign-in and usage limits.
  • Save and display recent analysis history.
  • Improve app stability, security, and user experience.
  • Provide premium or subscription features if enabled.

3. AI Processing

Content submitted for analysis may be sent to AI service providers, including OpenAI, in order to generate analysis results. Users should not submit sensitive personal information, confidential documents, passwords, or financial information.

4. Third-Party Services

TrustLens may use third-party services including:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Firebase services
  • OpenAI API
  • Google Play services
  • Google AdMob, if ads are enabled in the future

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell user personal information. Data may be shared only with service providers necessary to operate app features, comply with legal obligations, or protect the app and its users.

7. Children

TrustLens is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Medical, Legal, and Financial Disclaimer

TrustLens provides content credibility analysis only. It does not provide medical, legal, financial, or professional advice.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
